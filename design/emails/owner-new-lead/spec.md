# Owner new lead email (retired)

**Status:** Retired as of relay 2. Replaced by [owner-relay](../owner-relay/spec.md).

## What shipped

Immediate owner notification when a visitor completed exchange step 1, with optional phone on the exchange form. Subject and body referred to a new lead; included preheader and **Source** / **Received** style metadata in some builds.
