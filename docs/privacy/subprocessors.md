# Subprocessors (We Met)

Human-readable list aligned with the public privacy page (`src/components/landing/PrivacyPolicyContent.tsx`) and [`data-map.yml`](./data-map.yml). Outbound contact-exchange email is sent through **Cloudflare Email Service**.

| Subprocessor                 | Role                                                                                                            | Personal data they receive (when applicable)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Cloudflare**               | Hosts the app and consumer Workers; D1, R2, Queues, Turnstile, rate limiting, Workers Observability logs/traces | Data described under `processors: [Cloudflare]` in `data-map.yml` (account and card data in D1/R2, queue payloads with visitor leads, technical data such as IP for abuse protection, etc.)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Cloudflare Email Service** | Sends all contact-exchange and related outbound mail                                                            | For each outbound message: recipient address, sender address and display name, optional reply-to, subject, plain-text and HTML bodies, and attachments (including contact cards when visitor follow-up is on). Owner lead notifications always (visitor name, email, phone; append with job title, company, note; Reply-To visitor). Visitor follow-up only when `visitor_email_mode` is `direct` (card owner names, role, photo URL in body, vCard with owner email and name). Source: `src/email/cloudflare-sender.ts`, `src/followup-email-consumer/`. |
| **Google**                   | Google sign-in (OAuth ID token)                                                                                 | Google account subject, email, name, and email-verified flag during sign-in; we persist sub, email, and name only. Source: `src/app-auth/google-oauth.ts`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Google (People API)**      | Optional one-time contacts setup when the owner chooses Add We Met to my contacts                               | Google Contacts scope (see, edit, download, delete contacts). During setup only: searchContacts for our sending addresses (reads matched name and email), then create or update one We Met contact that persists in the owner Google Contacts until they delete it; we do not persist Google OAuth tokens. Source: `src/app-auth/google-people-api.ts`.                                                                                                                                                                                                   |

## Cloudflare Email Service — vendor retention and logging (published sources)

When the Cloudflare sending path is active, map rows `logs.cloudflare_email_sending_activity` and `logs.cloudflare_email_sending_preview` record what Cloudflare documents after handoff. `binding.consumer_send_email` stays `verified: false` until full message-body retention after delivery is published or confirmed.

| Topic                                        | What Cloudflare publishes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Map row                                                     |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Post-delivery event metadata**             | Activity log and `emailSendingAdaptive` events include sender, recipient, subject, message ID, status, authentication, and errors. Metrics and events are **retained 31 days**. Dashboard Activity log filters **30 minutes to 30 days** on the same data.                                                                                                                            | `logs.cloudflare_email_sending_activity` (`verified: true`) |
| **Optional message preview**                 | With **Email preview** on the sending domain, Activity log stores full HTML, text, headers, attachments, and raw RFC 5322 source for messages sent while the setting is on; previews are retained **about seven days**. New sending domains have Email preview on by default.                                                                                                         | `logs.cloudflare_email_sending_preview` (`verified: true`)  |
| **In-flight / post-delivery message bodies** | Email Service developer docs do not state a fixed retention period for full MIME after delivery. The [Cloudflare DPA](https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/) treats outbound mail as **Customer Content** with retention until agreement expiry/termination or when processing is no longer necessary (criteria, not a day count).                                      | `binding.consumer_send_email` (`verified: false`)           |
| **Configuration audit**                      | Email Sending configuration changes (domains, suppressions, etc.) are written to [account audit logs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/observability/audit-logs/) (18-month audit log retention per [review audit logs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/account/account-security/review-audit-logs/#retention)). Not mapped as message-content logging. | —                                                           |

**Source URLs (quoted in [DIDI-701](https://linear.app/didi-build/issue/DIDI-701) PR):**

- Activity log and preview: https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/observability/logs/
- Metrics / 31-day retention and event fields: https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/observability/metrics-analytics/
- Email preview domain setting: https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/configuration/domains/#email-preview
- Email Service audit log actions: https://developers.cloudflare.com/email-service/observability/audit-logs/
- DPA (Customer Content / Customer Logs categories): https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/

We Met does **not** use Cloudflare Email Routing for inbound mail; routing docs were reviewed only for completeness and are not in the map.
